# RosterBot v0.32 Phase 3 — Progressive Manual Onboarding

## 1. Baseline

Phase 3 began from clean source commit `fb308b9aeb46f3e93037b8eba4113b1ab3824a12` on `experiment/v0.32-ux-adoption`. The accepted Phase 2 front door, example roster, read-only duty dialog, roster browser, trust copy and returning-user disclosure were retained. Production was not accessed.

## 2. Before and after

Before Phase 3, one large setup surface exposed roster generation, scheduling-engine choices, role classification, role-effective history, alternation and annual leave together. A user had to understand pay and internal scheduling concepts before seeing their own roster.

The new sequence is:

1. Choose the real-world situation: master roster, regular pattern, or varying shifts.
2. Answer only the questions required by that branch.
3. Preview two weeks under **Does this look like your roster?**
4. Change setup or accept it.
5. After acceptance, optionally choose the current role to enable an honest pay estimate.

No arbitrary depot, roster or line can be accepted untouched. The controls begin with explicit `Choose…` options.

## 3. Field dependency analysis

| Input | First credible roster | Branch-only | Pay only | Later/advanced |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Applicable week/date | Required | — | — | Also anchors later history |
| Depot | Required for master roster | Master only | — | — |
| Roster | Required for master roster | Master only | — | — |
| Line | Required for master roster | Master only | — | — |
| Alternate depot/roster/line | — | Only when the user says they alternate | — | — |
| Regular work days/times | — | Regular-pattern users only | Supplies hours used later | Editable later |
| No-default/ad-hoc choice | — | Varying-shift users only | — | Fortnights entered later |
| Role/pay classification | No | — | Required for an authoritative dollar estimate | Full history remains available |
| Role-effective date | No | — | Needed for historical changes, not first current estimate | Employment & Pay Profile |
| Annual leave | No | — | May alter later calculation | Added after roster value |
| Experience/engine mode | No user-facing need | Replaced by situation choices | — | Internal representation retained |
| Display range/Quick mode | No | — | — | Existing configured tools retained |

Roster generation confirmed that the minimum master-roster inputs are the applicable week, depot, roster and line. Role facts do not participate in master-roster resolution.

## 4. Minimum normal master-roster inputs

The normal path now requires four explicit values after selecting **I’m on a master roster**:

- the week from which the selected line applies;
- depot;
- roster;
- line.

Alternation is still an explicit optional question. Its second arrangement is not shown until selected.

## 5. Deferred pay and employment

Role classification and role-effective history were removed from pre-preview onboarding. Acceptance deliberately creates an empty employment profile rather than silently asserting PB205.

After acceptance, Home presents **Want accurate pay estimates?** and explains why the role is needed. The small pay dialog asks for one current role. Its effective date is the saved roster’s starting week and can later be corrected through Employment & Pay Profile.

Until that step is complete, Home shows roster hours but says **Set up pay estimate** and **Pay role not set** instead of displaying a fallback dollar amount as authoritative.

## 6. Scheduling-mode presentation

The internal `official`, `pattern` and `adhoc` values remain unchanged for compatibility, but ordinary users now see:

- **I’m on a master roster**;
- **I work a regular pattern**;
- **My shifts vary**.

The underlying select remains available to existing legacy/quick workflows but is not exposed during first-run onboarding.

## 7. Alternation, manual and ad-hoc branches

The master path reveals depot/roster/line and an optional alternation control. The regular-pattern path hides all master-roster controls and reveals the established seven-day pattern editor. The varying-shifts path hides irrelevant master questions and explains that RosterBot will not invent default shifts.

Switching away from the master branch clears active alternation. Branch-specific inactive fields may remain in the device-local draft for convenience, but `currentSettings()` excludes them from the generated/accepted arrangement. Tests prove stale swap and pattern values cannot become authoritative after a branch switch.

## 8. Date and default wording

Master users are asked **Which week does this roster line apply from?** Supporting copy says that any date in the week is acceptable and shows the resulting WC Sunday. Manual users see **When should this schedule begin?**

Depot, roster and line now begin with explicit prompts. No preselected value implies that RosterBot already knows the driver’s position.

## 9. Roster preview

The existing preview remains the conversion boundary. It includes resolved dates, OR/work status, duties and times. **Change setup** returns to the progressive form without committing. **Use this as my roster/schedule** is the sole acceptance action.

An incorrect first preview followed by a second preview commits only the final selected arrangement.

## 10. Pay after roster

The saved roster works fully before pay setup. Current pay setup is one contextual question and one separately verified personal transaction. Full effective-dated role history, progression and PDT editing remain in Employment & Pay Profile for people who need them.

## 11. Draft and canonical results

- Situation and field changes: generation remains `1`; no canonical timeline; no Cloud write.
- Accepted initial roster: generation `1 → 2` exactly once with reason `accept-initial-roster`.
- Accepted setup has one timeline entry and no invented employment event.
- Current-role pay setup: generation `2 → 3` exactly once with reason `complete-current-pay-setup`.
- Dashboard/navigation after either transaction creates no duplicate generation.
- Draft is deleted only after successful roster acceptance.

The setup-draft validator now accepts partial progressive drafts while keeping the key device-local and outside backup, Cloud, IndexedDB personal surfaces and canonical fingerprints.

## 12. Abandonment and branch switching

Immediate close/background tests at the established `0, 5, 10, 25, 40, 75, 200 ms` boundaries remain green. A partial progressive draft reopens at generation 1, with no recovery state. The front-door action changes to **Continue setting up my roster** when a draft exists.

Refresh, close/reopen and branch switching retain relevant answers without allowing inactive branch fields to affect preview or acceptance.

## 13. Existing-user compatibility

An existing schema-5 fixture continues directly to Home with identical generation and fingerprint and no setup draft. Existing Roster History & Changes remains available and effective-dated. No initial-onboarding simplification was applied to later roster-history editing.

Legacy sessions containing role fields retain them. New sessions may omit the now-separate role fields without being classified as malformed or forcing old sessions to migrate at startup.

## 14. Accessibility and mobile

The new flow uses ordered H2/H3 headings, an explicitly labelled choice group, `aria-pressed` selection state, visible focus, minimum accepted touch sizing, associated instructions and an alert region for pay errors. Focus moves to the next required date after a branch choice. The pay dialog uses the Phase 1 dialog controller and restores focus on close.

At 390 × 844 and 200% text, the choice cards reflow to one column with zero horizontal overflow. The accepted Phase 6 safe-area, modal and WebKit keyboard contracts remain unchanged.

## 15. Adoption measurements

| Measure | Phase 0A baseline | Phase 3 ordinary master path |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Required decisions before preview | Approximately 8 | 5, including the situation choice |
| Explicit values before preview | 7 plus alternation | 4 plus optional alternation |
| Pay questions before roster value | 2 | 0 |
| Internal/unfamiliar concepts before preview | Approximately 10–15 compressed terms | 0 scheduling-engine terms |
| Phone burden | One long mixed-purpose form | Two short progressive sections |
| Preview to acceptance | 1 action | 1 action |
| Accepted roster to current pay estimate | Coupled before preview | Open pay setup, choose role, save |

The reduction comes from removing unnecessary decisions, not from accepting silent defaults.

## 16. Regression results

The final candidate passed `302/302` persistence/static tests and `12/12` browser matrices: Phase 1 transaction/termination and dialogs; Phase 2 front door/demo/browse and PWA structure; Phase 3 progressive onboarding and offline shell; Phase 4 reconnect; Phase 5 controlled update; Phase 6 mobile standalone; legacy import/migration; schema-5 restore/reload; encrypted unlock; and foundational startup diagnostics. The candidate build verifies with 49 runtime assets and 46 offline-required assets.

The offline-shell and schema-5 restore matrices were repeated alone after parallel Chromium runs produced one transient loopback request and one staging calendar-status GET respectively. Both isolated authoritative reruns passed with zero network requests in the cold-offline case and zero production contacts in the Cloud case. No production request was made by any Phase 3 harness.

## 17. Files changed

Phase 3 changes the setup and pay presentation in `index.html`, progressive setup and transactional pay handling in `js/20-roster-ui.js`, dashboard truthfulness/navigation in `js/70-navigation.js`, draft validation in `js/01-persistence-registry.js`, focused CSS in `css/rosterbot.css`, browser/static tests, package scripts and this report. Generated build identity and service-worker files are updated only after tests settle.

## 18. Known limitations

- The master-roster path remains manual; RosterBot does not yet ingest weekly or daily published assignments.
- Current-role setup intentionally uses the accepted roster’s starting week. Users with relevant earlier role history must add/correct it in Employment & Pay Profile.
- Ad-hoc users still need to enter individual fortnights later because no reliable default schedule exists.
- Annual leave remains a post-roster task and is not yet introduced by a dedicated contextual prompt.

## 19. Phase 4 opportunities recorded, not implemented

- A compact four-week forward Home calendar.
- Stronger Today/Tomorrow and next-Off-Roster hierarchy.
- Lightweight read-only detail before the configured actual-day editor.
- Contextual leave addition after roster acceptance.
- Projected/published/actual provenance and change awareness when authorised roster ingestion exists.

No configured Home redesign was made in Phase 3.

## 20. Recommendation

Proceed to Phase 4 only after human beta review confirms that the new master-roster, regular-pattern, varying-shift and deferred-pay paths feel clear on a phone and that the explicit empty defaults create confidence without unnecessary effort.
